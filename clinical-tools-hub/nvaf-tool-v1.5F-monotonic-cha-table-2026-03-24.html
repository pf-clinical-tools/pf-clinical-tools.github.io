CHA₂DS₂-VASc + ORBIT with frailty-informed interpretation and drug-specific annual risk translation. For clinicians only.

1. Inputs

Tick all that apply. Outputs update automatically.

CHA₂DS₂-VASc – Stroke risk (no OAC)
CHA₂DS₂-VASc 0 Stroke risk 0.7% / year

Thresholds are sex-specific: men 0 and women 1 (sex only) are below threshold; men 1 / women 2 = consider; men ≥2 / women ≥3 = OAC usually recommended in most patients unless prognosis, goals-of-care, or bleeding context alter the decision.

ORBIT – Major bleeding risk (on OAC)
Derived automatically from CHA₂DS₂-VASc to ensure internal consistency.
Male Hb <130 g/L; female Hb <120 g/L
ORBIT 0 Bleeding risk ≈2.5% / year (low)

Use ORBIT to identify and mitigate bleeding risk; do not use it alone to deny OAC where stroke risk is above the usual treatment threshold.
